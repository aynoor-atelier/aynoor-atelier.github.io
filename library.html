The Aynoor Library

The institutional archive of Aynoor Atelier.

The Aynoor Library is the institutional archive of Aynoor Atelier. It preserves the governing documents, publications, historical records, and foundational knowledge that define the continuity of the Institution.

The Library does not exist to collect information. It exists to preserve institutional understanding.
